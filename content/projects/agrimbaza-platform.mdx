Built a benchmark and public leaderboard that compares Kinyarwanda ASR, TTS and LLM models. ASR is scored automatically with WER and CER. A panel of human raters scores TTS and LLM outputs, with hidden gold items and inter-rater agreement checks for quality control. Teams submit model predictions and receive a ranked, published result.
