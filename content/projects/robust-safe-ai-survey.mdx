An in-depth review of adversarial vulnerabilities in multilingual and low-resource language models, classifying attack methods and analysing their implications for model safety and reliability.

Part of the work examines how prompt injection affects the robustness of multilingual LLMs — specifically their capacity to bypass safety mechanisms and produce harmful or unintended output under adversarial conditions.
